FeelS is a local-first Android app for naming how you feel. Spin a feelings wheel, log a check-in with intensity and an optional note, and review a 30-day heatmap plus weekly mood widgets.

Your check-ins stay on the device in an encrypted database. FeelS does not send emotion data to a server.

Features:
• Feelings wheel with primary, secondary, and tertiary layers
• Optional notes and intensity
• History, heatmap, and home-screen widgets
• Breathing shortcut and gentle grounding after high-distress logs
• Daily reminders you can turn off or reschedule
• JSON export and import so you can move logs to another phone

FeelS is not medical care, diagnosis, or crisis support.
